## Hello, I’m Marsha.

I’m a software developer who enjoys turning an idea into something clear, useful, and real. I’m especially interested in web development and the small technical decisions that make a product easier to understand and maintain.

I learn best by building. My current projects include this Jekyll portfolio and a Python/FastAPI service for handling portfolio contact messages. Together, they have helped me practise frontend structure, backend APIs, deployment, configuration, and documentation.

## What I work with

- **Languages:** Python, HTML, CSS/Sass
- **Frameworks and tools:** FastAPI, Jekyll, Bootstrap
- **Workflow:** Git, GitHub, GitHub Actions
