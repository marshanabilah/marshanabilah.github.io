# Portfolio design direction

## Design read

Personal portfolio for AI and data-engineering recruiters and technical collaborators, grounded in an editorial research-document visual language.

Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

## Identity

The portfolio should feel analytical, credible, and personal. Research and working systems are the primary evidence. Production experience supports that story rather than competing with it.

## Visual language

- Use editorial hierarchy to make substantial work easy to scan and read.
- Keep the page calm enough for technical material, with deliberate variation between research, applied projects, and professional experience.
- Use one restrained teal accent to guide attention without making every element compete.
- Keep motion limited to useful hover and focus feedback.

## Content principles

- Prefer concrete methods, tools, and outcomes over broad claims.
- Give the strongest AI and data work the clearest visual priority.
- Keep all statistics and experience details traceable to Marsha's CV or project repositories.
